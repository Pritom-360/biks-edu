Admission Information

Step-by-Step Admission Process

  1. Collect the admission form from the school office or download it online.
  2. Submit the filled-out form along with required documents (birth certificate, photos).
  3. Attend a parent-teacher interaction session with your child.
  4. Receive the admission confirmation and complete the payment process.
