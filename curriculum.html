Our Curriculum

NCTB Framework for Parents

We follow the National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB) of Bangladesh as our core framework. This ensures that our students receive a nationally recognized and comprehensive education.

Our approach simplifies the NCTB guidelines for young learners, focusing on foundational literacy, numeracy, and social skills. We make learning interactive and joyful, ensuring that children meet national standards while developing a genuine love for knowledge.

Innovative Learning Methodologies

Phenomenon-Based Learning (PhBL)

Instead of studying isolated subjects, students explore real-world phenomena. A topic like "Water" can involve science (evaporation), math (measurement), art (painting), and language (stories about rivers), creating a holistic and engaging learning experience.

Project-Based Learning (PBL)

Children work on hands-on projects over a period of time. From building a small garden to creating a class storybook, PBL helps develop critical thinking, collaboration, and problem-solving skills in a practical and fun way.
