Our Facilities

Spacious Indoor Playground

A safe, clean, and vibrant indoor play area with soft flooring and age-appropriate toys to encourage physical activity regardless of the weather.

Multimedia Classrooms

All classrooms are equipped with digital tools to make learning interactive and fun. We use audio-visual aids to explain concepts effectively.

Safety & Security

The entire campus is monitored by CCTV cameras. We have trained staff and a secure entry/exit system to ensure your child's safety at all times.

Student-Teacher Ratio

1:15

We maintain a low student-teacher ratio to ensure personalized attention and support for every child's individual learning journey.
